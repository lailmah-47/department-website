Admissions

Join our department and advance your career in computer science

Degree Programs

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Duration: 4 years (120 credit hours)

Our B.S. program provides comprehensive training in computer science fundamentals, algorithms, software engineering, and systems. Students choose specializations in AI, cybersecurity, or software engineering.

Typical Schedule: Full-time on campus | Start Date: Fall & Spring

Master of Science in Computer Science

Duration: 2 years (36 credit hours)

Advanced program for graduates seeking specialization in machine learning, distributed systems, cybersecurity, or computer vision. Includes thesis and research requirements.

Typical Schedule: Full-time or Part-time | Start Date: Fall only

Graduate Certificate in AI & Machine Learning

Duration: 1 year (12 credit hours)

Professional certificate program focused on practical machine learning and AI applications. Ideal for working professionals seeking to upskill in emerging technologies.

Typical Schedule: Part-time evening classes | Start Date: Fall & Spring

Graduate Certificate in Cybersecurity

Duration: 1 year (12 credit hours)

Intensive certificate program covering network security, cryptography, and threat analysis. Prepares students for cybersecurity roles in industry.

Typical Schedule: Part-time evening classes | Start Date: Fall & Spring

Admission Criteria

Bachelor's Program Requirements

  • High school diploma or equivalent (GED)
  • Minimum GPA: 3.0 (unweighted)
  • SAT score: 1200+ or ACT score: 28+
  • Completion of 4 years of mathematics (including Calculus recommended)
  • Completion of 2 years of laboratory science
  • Demonstrated interest in STEM fields

Master's Program Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution
  • Minimum undergraduate GPA: 3.2
  • GRE scores (Quantitative: 160+, Analytical Writing: 3.5+)
  • Strong background in computer science (programming, algorithms, discrete math)
  • Statement of purpose (500-750 words)
  • Three letters of recommendation
  • Current resume/CV

Graduate Certificate Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution
  • Relevant work experience or technical background
  • Brief statement of professional goals (250 words)
  • One letter of recommendation

Application Process

Step 1: Submit Online Application

Complete the online application form through our admissions portal. Include your personal information, academic history, and program preferences.

Required: Application fee ($75)

Step 2: Submit Official Transcripts

Request official transcripts from all colleges and universities you have attended. Transcripts should be sent directly to our admissions office.

Deadline: Within 2 weeks of application submission

Step 3: Standardized Test Scores

Submit official SAT/ACT scores (for undergraduate) or GRE scores (for graduate). Scores must be sent directly from the testing agency.

Minimum Scores: See admission criteria above

Step 4: Submit Additional Documents

Submit letters of recommendation, statement of purpose, and resume through the application portal. Ensure all documents are in PDF format.

Deadline: Same as transcript deadline

Step 5: Application Review

Our admissions committee will review your complete application. We consider academic performance, test scores, and demonstrated interest in the field.

Timeline: 4-6 weeks after submission

Step 6: Admission Decision

You will receive an admission decision via email. Admitted students will receive enrollment instructions and may be contacted for additional information.

Types: Admitted, Waitlisted, or Not Admitted

Step 7: Enrollment

Confirm your enrollment by submitting the enrollment deposit. Complete your student information and select courses for your first semester.

Deposit: $500 (applied to first semester tuition)
